## Overview

Android application to manage medical appointments, track medication, find nearby pharmacies and hospitals, and receive medication reminders.

## What I did

- Built an Android application with Kotlin and Android Studio.
- Implemented appointment management, medication tracking and reminders, with local storage in SQLite.
- Added the location of nearby pharmacies and hospitals.

## Technologies

Kotlin, Android Studio, SQLite
